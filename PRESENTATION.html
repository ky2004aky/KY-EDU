1. Context & Motivation

Problem Statement & Background

🚨 The Aspirant's Challenge

  • Millions of students pass 10th, 12th, ITI, Polytechnic, B.Tech, Agriculture, or Law every year.
  • Students struggle to identify which central/state recruitments they are legally eligible for.
  • Complex eligibility relaxation rules (OBC/SC/ST/PwD) lead to invalid form applications and rejected fees.

⚠️ Flaws in Existing Portals

  • Extreme Ad Clutter: 10+ spam banners, popups, and clickbait download links per page.
  • No Stream Categorization: Notifications dumped in chronological lists without academic filtering.
  • Zero Preparation Insight: No guidance on high-yield chapters, syllabus weights, or booklists.

3. System Goals

Key Objectives of KY EDU

🎯 1. Stream Discovery

Connect candidates to verified exams based strictly on their academic streams (10th, 12th PCM, Commerce, Agriculture, ITI, B.Tech, Law, Nursing).

⚡ 2. Smart Search

Instant omnibar with auto-suggestions and tokenized search matching titles, posts, qualifications, and pay scales without false negatives.

⚖️ 3. Eligibility Calculator

Auto-evaluate candidate qualification, age ceilings, reservation relaxations (OBC/SC/ST/PwD), and final-year appearing rules.

📚 4. Standard Booklists

Equip aspirants with authoritative Indian textbooks (M. Laxmikanth, RS Aggarwal, Spectrum) and high-yield scoring chapters.

🔒 5. Protected Admin

Isolate exam updates and circular broadcasting behind multi-factor PIN (123456) and Bearer token authentication.

📊 6. Multi-View Formats

Offer 4 view layouts: Cards Grid (Flipkart Style), Sarkari Table, Fast Scanner, and Stage Pipeline Timeline.

7. Administration & Control

Protected Admin Portal Modules

🔐 1. Multi-Tier Authentication

  • Quick 6-Digit PIN access (123456) for fast access.
  • SuperAdmin credentials (admin@kyedu.in).
  • Automatic generation of Bearer tokens for all CRUD requests.
  • Unauthorized mutating calls return 403 Forbidden.

📊 2. Executive Analytics Dashboard

  • Live counters for Active Exams, Pathways, and Live Circulars.
  • Student department breakdown distribution chart.
  • Recent activity audit feed for notices and exams.

✏️ 3. Full CRUD Management

  • Create new exams with multi-stage exam tiers and salaries.
  • Modify existing posts, syllabus topics, and eligibility.
  • Delete outdated or expired notifications from MongoDB.

📢 4. Live Notice Ticker Broadcaster

  • Broadcast time-sensitive circulars (Admit Cards, Results).
  • Directly updates the amber announcement ticker on the homepage.
  • Export registered student data to CSV format.
