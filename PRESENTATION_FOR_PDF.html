1. Context & Motivation
Problem Statement & Background
KY EDU

🚨 The Aspirant's Challenge

  • Severe Stream Confusion: Millions of students completing 10th, 12th, ITI, Polytechnic, Agriculture, Law, or Nursing do not know which government recruitments match their degree.
  • Application Fee Loss: Misunderstanding age relaxation rules (OBC/SC/ST) and final-year appearing norms leads to rejected forms.
  • Neglected Non-Traditional Streams: Vocational ITI trades, polytechnic diplomas, and agricultural graduates lack dedicated guidance.

⚠️ Existing Portal Flaws (e.g. Sarkari Result)

  • Excessive Advertisement Clutter: 10+ spam banners, popups, and clickbait download links per page.
  • Zero Academic Filtering: Notifications are dumped in flat chronological lists without academic stream filtering.
  • No High-Yield Booklists: Aspirants are left searching on YouTube/Telegram for syllabus weights and standard Indian books.

3. System Goals
Key Objectives of KY EDU
KY EDU

🎯 1. Stream Discovery

Connect candidates to verified exams based strictly on academic streams (10th, 12th PCM, Commerce, Agriculture, ITI, B.Tech, Law, Nursing).

⚡ 2. Real-Time Search

Instant omnibar with live auto-suggestions and tokenized search matching titles, posts, qualifications, and pay scales without false negatives.

⚖️ 3. Eligibility Calculator

Auto-evaluate candidate qualification, age ceilings, reservation relaxations (OBC/SC/ST/PwD), and final-year appearing rules.

📚 4. Standard Booklists

Equip aspirants with authoritative textbooks (M. Laxmikanth, RS Aggarwal, Spectrum) and high-yield scoring chapters.

🔒 5. Protected Admin

Isolate exam updates and circular broadcasting behind multi-factor PIN (123456) and Bearer token authentication.

📊 6. Multi-View Formats

Offer 4 view layouts: Cards Grid (Flipkart Style), Sarkari Table, Fast Scanner, and Stage Pipeline Timeline.

7. Administration & Control
Protected Admin Portal Modules
KY EDU

🔐 1. Multi-Tier Authentication

  • Quick 6-Digit PIN access (123456) for rapid access.
  • SuperAdmin credentials (admin@kyedu.in).
  • Automatic generation of Bearer tokens for all CRUD requests.
  • Unauthorized mutating calls return 403 Forbidden.

📊 2. Executive Analytics Dashboard

  • Live counters for Active Exams, Pathways, and Live Circulars.
  • Student department breakdown distribution chart.
  • Recent activity audit feed for notices and exams.

✏️ 3. Full CRUD Management

  • Create new exams with multi-stage exam tiers and salaries.
  • Modify existing posts, syllabus topics, and eligibility.
  • Delete outdated or expired notifications from MongoDB.

📢 4. Live Notice Ticker Broadcaster

  • Broadcast time-sensitive circulars (Admit Cards, Results).
  • Directly updates the amber announcement ticker on the homepage.
  • Export registered student data to CSV format.
